About Me

I am a second-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Professors Tom Goldstein and Matthias Zwicker. My research focuses on efficient and reliable 3D/4D reconstruction and generation, including compression and distillation methods for Gaussian Splatting and diffusion-based priors that enable reconstruction from sparse or degraded imagery. I also worked on multimodal biometric recognition under severe operating conditions. I am currently an Applied Science Intern at Amazon, building photorealistic simulation environments for training embodied AI by reconstructing physics-ready 3D digital twins from real-world sensor data.

My research is supported through the IARPA WRIVA and BRIAR programs and shaped by collaborations across academia and industry, including prior internships at Systems & Technology Research (STR) and my current role at Amazon. I co-organized the inaugural SPAR-3D Workshop at CVPR 2026 on security and adversarial robustness in 3D generative vision models, and previously mentored undergraduate researchers in the FIRE: Capital One Machine Learning program. I received my B.S./M.S. in Computer Science from UMD in 2024.

Research Highlights BibTeX References

SpeeDe3DGS: Speedy Deformable 3D Gaussian Splatting with Temporal Pruning and Motion Grouping
CVPR 2026
PAPER CODE WEBSITE
Boost dynamic Gaussian Splatting rendering speed from 20 to 276 FPS using temporal sensitivity pruning and groupwise SE(3) motion distillation, all while preserving the superior image quality of per-Gaussian neural motion.
Allen Tu*, Haiyang Ying*, Alex Hanson, Yonghan Lee, Tom Goldstein, Matthias Zwicker
SplatSuRe: Selective Super-Resolution for Multi-view Consistent 3D Gaussian Splatting
CVPR 2026
PAPER CODE WEBSITE
Enhance 3D Gaussian Splatting from low-resolution training images by injecting super-resolution only in regions lacking high-frequency supervision from other views, yielding sharper, more faithful reconstructions while preserving multi-view consistency.
Pranav Asthana, Alex Hanson, Allen Tu, Tom Goldstein, Matthias Zwicker, Amitabh Varshney
TransFIRA: Transfer Learning for Face Image Recognizability Assessment
FG 2026 (Oral)
PAPER CODE TALK WEBSITE
Learn explainable, encoder-specific recognizability assessment from embedding geometry, enabling principled filtering, recognizability-aware template aggregation, state-of-the-art face image quality assessment performance, and generalization to body recognizability assessment.
Allen Tu, Kartik Narayan, Joshua Gleason, Jennifer Xu, Matthew Meyn, Tom Goldstein, Vishal Patel
PUP 3D-GS: Principled Uncertainty Pruning for 3D Gaussian Splatting
CVPR 2025
PAPER CODE WEBSITE
Prune 90% of primitives from any pretrained 3D Gaussian Splatting model using a mathematically principled sensitivity score, more than tripling rendering speed while retaining more salient foreground information and higher visual fidelity than previous techniques at a substantially higher compression ratio.
Alex Hanson*, Allen Tu*, Vasu Singla, Mayuka Jayawardhana, Matthias Zwicker, Tom Goldstein
Speedy-Splat: Fast 3D Gaussian Splatting with Sparse Pixels and Sparse Primitives
CVPR 2025
PAPER CODE WEBSITE
Accelerate 3D Gaussian Splatting rendering speed by 2× for free by accurately localizing primitives during rasterization and over 6× in total by pruning the scene by more than 90% during training, providing a significantly higher speedup than existing techniques while maintaining competitive image quality.
Alex Hanson, Allen Tu, Geng Lin, Vasu Singla, Matthias Zwicker, Tom Goldstein
